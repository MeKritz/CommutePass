Your Daily Commute, Simplified

CommutePass is an AI-powered transportation platform designed to make everyday travel simple, affordable and hassle-free.

Why CommutePass?

Daily commuters often face difficulties during peak hours and rainy weather. Ride cancellations, unavailable drivers, surge pricing and repeated payment processes make daily transportation inconvenient.

CommutePass provides a smarter transportation experience using subscriptions, ride credits and artificial intelligence.

Flexible Payment Options

CommutePass

Purchase a weekly or monthly subscription and receive digital ride credits.

After every completed ride, the required credits are automatically deducted from your balance.

Manual Payment

Users who do not want a subscription can book a ride normally and pay individually after each ride.

AI-Powered Features

Voice AI Assistant

Book and manage rides using voice commands.

Driver-Rider Matching

Matches riders with suitable drivers using route, timing and availability.

Demand Prediction

Predicts transportation demand during peak hours.

Cancellation Prediction

Detects possible cancellations and helps assign backup drivers.

Route Optimization

Finds efficient routes for riders and drivers.

Agentic AI

Helps schedule rides, check credits and manage bookings automatically.
